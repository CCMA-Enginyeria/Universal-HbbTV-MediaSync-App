# Google Play Store — Release notes (What's new)

Version **1.8.0**. One block per supported language, wrapped in its Play Store
locale tag. Character limit: **500 characters** per language (Play Console
"What's new" field). Also used for the App Store "What's New" and the Meta
Horizon Store build.

<en-GB>
What's new in 1.8.0:
• Voice control with Siri and Gemini: turn on the TV audio, choose the language or audio description, hear what the programme offers and stop it
• Playback stops right away when you stop it with your voice in the background
Thanks for using Universal MediaSync!
</en-GB>
