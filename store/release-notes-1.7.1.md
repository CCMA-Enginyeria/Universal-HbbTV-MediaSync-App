# Google Play Store — Release notes (What's new)

Version **1.7.1**. One block per supported language, wrapped in its Play Store
locale tag. Character limit: **500 characters** per language (Play Console
"What's new" field). Also used for the App Store "What's New" and the first
Meta Horizon Store build.

• Fixes for synchronized playback of DASH content on iPhone and iPad
